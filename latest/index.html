SLAMLab

SLAMLab is an easy-to-use, easy-to-integrate framework for localization and mapping.
Documentation YouTube Contact

Flexible sensor setup

Use the sensors you have: lidar, IMU and GNSS together, or any subset of them. Lidar+IMU, lidar+GNSS, IMU+GNSS and lidar only are all supported.

Proven accuracy

SLAMLab won first place in the COMFORT Challenge on the GrandTour Benchmark, held at the IROS 2026 workshop co-organized by ETH Zürich and the University of Oxford.

Easy integration

Process recorded data offline, run SLAMLab as a ROS 2 node, embed the C++ libraries in your own application, or run it on an iPad.

Any platform

Carry it handheld or in a backpack, mount it on a car or a drone, or put it on robots and heavy machinery: SLAMLab works on the platform you use.
